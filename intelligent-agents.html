Intelligent Agents

Portfolio evidence, practical activities, project evaluation and reflective learning from the Intelligent Agents module.

Module Overview

The Intelligent Agents module explores the motivations for agent-based computing, established agent models and architectures, and the application of intelligent systems to real-world problems. It also considers contemporary research in intelligent agent systems alongside the technical, legal, social, ethical and professional issues involved in their design and deployment.
